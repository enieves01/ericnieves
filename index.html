The process

Assess

We start with a free movement screen and a real conversation about your history, your goals, and what's gotten in the way before. No judgment — just a starting line.

02

Build

You get a program built for your body and your schedule — strength, conditioning, and recovery that fit a real life, not a magazine cover. Adjusted week to week.

Show up and put in the work. Eric handles the coaching, the form checks, and the accountability so all you have to bring is effort. Results follow.

About Eric

Eric Nieves has spent more than a decade helping people in the Lake Norman and greater Charlotte area get strong, move better, and stick with it. He came up the hard way — overweight, intimidated by the gym, convinced he wasn't "an athlete." Learning to train changed his life, and now he does the same for his clients.

His style is simple: meet you where you are, push you a little further than you'd push yourself, and never let the plan get more complicated than it needs to be. He trains clients in person around Lake Norman and online anywhere — same attention, same standards.
