Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Where is in-person training located?

In-person coaching is available in the Lake Norman area. Exact location details are provided when an assessment is arranged.

Can I train if I have a previous injury?

Training can often be modified around previous injuries and movement limitations. Medical clearance may be requested when appropriate.

What happens during the free assessment?

We'll discuss your goals, training history, previous injuries, current routine, and what has been getting in the way. From there, we'll determine the most useful next step for your training.

How often should I train?

That depends on your goals, schedule, recovery, and training experience. After your assessment, we'll build a realistic weekly structure you can actually maintain.

Is coaching only strength training?

Strength training is the foundation, but coaching can also include home mobility work, cardiovascular education and programming, and practical nutrition consultation based on your goals and needs.
