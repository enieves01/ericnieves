A plan built around you

Your coaching begins with understanding where you are now and continues to evolve as you make progress.

01

Assess

We discuss your goals, training history, previous injuries, current routine, and the challenges you have faced in your fitness journey. Your assessment may also include basic strength, mobility, balance, posture, and movement testing. Optional body-composition measurements, including body-fat percentage and skeletal muscle mass, may also be recorded to establish a baseline and monitor progress over time.

02

Plan

Your program is built around your body, experience, schedule, available equipment, and goals—not copied from a generic template. Your plan may include strength training, cardiovascular conditioning, mobility work, and nutrition-focused action steps.

03

Coach

We refine your technique, monitor your progress, adjust your program, and keep you accountable. Your plan evolves as your strength, confidence, and abilities improve.

Meet Your Coach

I'm Eric Nieves, a Certified Personal Trainer and owner of Eric Nieves Coaching. I specialize in corrective exercise, strength development, and helping people train confidently while accounting for previous injuries and movement limitations.

With a B.S. in Biology, experience in rehabilitation-focused settings, and certifications in personal training, corrective exercise, and nutrition coaching, I take a practical approach that connects movement quality with effective strength and fitness training.

I work with a wide range of clients, including people beginning their fitness journeys, individuals returning to training after injury or time away, and athletes pursuing performance goals. My coaching combines strength training, mobility, cardiovascular conditioning, nutrition guidance, and accountability to create results that are both effective and sustainable.

Training does not need to be unnecessarily complicated. My goal is to give you the right plan, teach you how to execute it confidently, and help you become stronger and more capable over time.

Frequently asked questions

Where is in-person training located?

In-person coaching is available in the Lake Norman area. Exact location details are provided when an assessment is scheduled.

Do I need previous gym experience?

No. Coaching is adapted to your current experience, ability, and comfort level.

Can I train if I have a previous injury?

Training can often be modified around previous injuries and movement limitations. Medical clearance may be requested when appropriate.

What is included with online coaching?

Online coaching includes personalized programming, exercise demonstrations, form review, regular check-ins, cardio and nutrition guidance, and messaging support.

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. The introductory assessment allows us to discuss your goals, evaluate your needs, and determine whether coaching is a good fit.
