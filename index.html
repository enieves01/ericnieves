Your coach

Coach. Lifter.
In your corner.

Eric Nieves has spent more than a decade helping people in the Lake Norman and greater Charlotte area get strong, move better, and stick with it. He came up the hard way — overweight, intimidated by the gym, convinced he wasn't "an athlete." Learning to train changed his life, and now he does the same for his clients.

His style is simple: meet you where you are, push you a little further than you'd push yourself, and never let the plan get more complicated than it needs to be. Whether you're chasing your first pull-up, rebuilding after an injury, or trying to drop weight for good, you'll get a coach who actually pays attention.

He trains clients in person around Lake Norman and online anywhere — same attention, same standards.
