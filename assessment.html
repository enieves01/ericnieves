Start With a Free Assessment

The free assessment is an opportunity to discuss your goals, identify your current needs, and determine whether coaching is the right fit for you. There is no obligation to sign up.

What does the assessment involve?

We'll begin with a conversation about your:

  • Fitness goals
  • Training experience
  • Current exercise routine
  • Previous injuries or movement limitations
  • Schedule, available equipment, and potential barriers
  • Expectations for personal training or online coaching

For in-person clients, the assessment may also include introductory checks of posture, mobility, balance, movement quality, and basic strength. Movements are selected based on your comfort level and training history.

The assessment is not a medical evaluation or diagnosis. You may be asked to obtain clearance from a qualified healthcare provider when appropriate.

Who is the assessment for?

The assessment is designed for people who:

  • Are beginning or returning to a fitness routine
  • Want to build strength with proper instruction
  • Have completed rehabilitation and want guidance returning to exercise
  • Need training modifications for previous injuries or movement limitations
  • Feel unsure about what exercises or program to follow
  • Want greater structure, accountability, and consistency
  • Are working toward general fitness, body-composition, or athletic goals
  • Are considering in-person personal training or online coaching

You do not need to be in shape or have previous gym experience before scheduling.

How long does it take?

Most assessments take approximately 45 minutes to one hour. The exact length may vary depending on your goals, training history, and whether movement or body-composition measurements are included.

Where does it take place?

In-person assessments are completed at my private training studio in the Lake Norman area. The studio provides a private, one-on-one environment without the crowds or distractions of a commercial gym. The exact address and arrival instructions will be provided after your appointment is confirmed.

Virtual assessments are also available for people interested in online coaching. These are completed through a video call and focus on your goals, history, current routine, available equipment, and programming needs.

What should I wear or bring?

Wear comfortable athletic clothing that allows you to move freely, along with supportive athletic shoes. You may also want to bring:

  • Water
  • Information about previous injuries or surgeries
  • Relevant exercise or rehabilitation recommendations
  • A list of your current fitness goals
  • Questions about training or coaching
  • Any medical clearance you have been asked to obtain

You do not need to prepare for a full workout. Any movement completed during the assessment will be introductory and adjusted to your ability and comfort level.

Ready to get started?

Schedule your free assessment to discuss your goals, evaluate your needs, and determine the best next step for your training.
